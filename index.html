Extra 03 — Custom songTrack 01

This one is for you.

Every person has a soundtrack. So why shouldn't their birthday have one?

Every experience can include one custom song, written around the recipient — their personality, a memory the two of you share, an inside joke, the music they already love. It's presented as part of the story, not dropped in as a digital afterthought.

That's Guitar Motif you can hear playing now — our default track, looping quietly in the background wherever you go on the site until you pause it. This ticket is the real, playable control for it: press play to take it over yourself, or press ‹ › to switch to one of three lyrical demo songs, so you can hear the range of what's possible. Whatever you land on keeps playing as you keep scrolling — pause it and it stays paused everywhere.

If a recognizable voice is ever involved, we ask for permission first, always. And we won't promise a professional studio recording unless that's specifically what's arranged.

Questions

Things people usually ask.

I know all of these by heart.
Can I choose the main gift?

Yes. Tell us exactly what you'd like the main gift to be, and we'll build the rest of the experience around it.

Can you choose the gift for me?

Yes. Tell us about the recipient and we'll curate it from there.

Can I choose between the two philosophies?

Yes — Humble Curiosity, Enchanting Expansion, or a mix of both.

What if I don't know which philosophy fits?

Tell us about the recipient and we'll recommend an approach.

Is the song really customized?

Yes. The song is created around the recipient and whatever information you give us.

Can I request a specific type of gift?

Yes, subject to availability and the budget of the package you've chosen.

Do you deliver?

Delivery details will go here once finalized.

Can I order for a birthday?

Yes. Birthdays are the primary occasion, but the experience can be adapted for other occasions too.

Do I need to tell you the box size or how to pack everything?

No — that part is entirely on us. Once you tell us the main gift and anything else you'd like included, we decide the box size, the packaging and how it's all arranged inside, based on what's actually going in. You just answer a few quick questions if we need anything more; the assembly is a mystery we handle ourselves.

What if I don't want to chat on WhatsApp or Instagram?

Nothing you have to do — really. about the person you're gifting, right here on the site, and place your order that way instead. Almost everything we need is covered in those 10 questions, so there's nothing left to worry about. Or, if you'd rather talk it through, message us directly on WhatsApp or Instagram, whichever you prefer, or hop on a call with us.
